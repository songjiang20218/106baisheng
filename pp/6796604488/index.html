Policy summary

  • AI sharing is optional. Content is sent only when you actively use an AI feature and, where required, after you select “Agree and Continue” on the separate AI Data Sharing authorization screen.
  • If you select “Not Now,” AI content is not sent. Non-AI, on-device document tools remain available.
  • AI requests pass through office.offerapp.ai and OpenRouter, which may route them to an OpenAI or Microsoft Azure model endpoint.
  • You can withdraw authorization in Me > AI Data & Privacy. Withdrawal stops new AI transmissions but does not by itself delete previously processed data.
  • We do not use document content for advertising, sale of personal information, or cross-app tracking.

02 / LOCAL PROCESSING

Documents, local data, and device permissions

Documents you create, open, scan, import, or edit may remain on your device or in storage locations you choose. Viewing, basic editing, conversion, and other non-AI tools do not require us to send document content to an AI provider.

Permissions used only when you request the related feature

  • Camera: scan documents or capture pages.
  • Photos: import images or save an output you choose.
  • Microphone: record dictation, meetings, or voice notes.
  • Speech Recognition: convert speech to text. Depending on device capability and settings, audio may be processed on-device or by Apple Speech.
  • Files: open, import, edit, export, or save files you select.

You can deny or later change permissions in iOS Settings. Denial affects only the related feature.

03 / AI FEATURES

AI features and data sharing

We collect and transmit AI content only when you deliberately start the corresponding function. Depending on the feature and your selection, a request may include:

  • text, prompts, topics, instructions, drafts, and email content;
  • language, tone, length, audience, format, and output settings;
  • the file name and content of files you actively select;
  • PDF or Word document text and spreadsheet cells;
  • ChatPDF PDF content and your questions;
  • meeting transcript text, title, transcript segments, action items, and bookmarks;
  • an App-specific device identifier where used for request integrity, rate limiting, or abuse prevention; and
  • IP address, request time, request path, user-agent information, and security logs generated when the request reaches our server.

AI outputs may include writing, rewrites, email drafts, summaries, extracted information, ChatPDF answers, spreadsheet analysis, presentation outlines, meeting minutes, action items, and bookmarks.

Review before sending. Selected content may already contain personal, confidential, or sensitive information. Do not submit it unless necessary for the feature.

05 / REMOTE DOCUMENT EDITING

Nextcloud and Collabora remote editing

If you choose remote editing, the selected file is uploaded to our Nextcloud instance at cloud.offerapp.ai. Collabora processes the active editing session and saves changes back to Nextcloud. Collabora has no separate persistent document volume in our current production configuration; the persistent copy is held by Nextcloud.

Files remain until you delete them or ask us to delete them. A normal deletion moves a file to the Nextcloud trash bin. You may empty the trash bin to permanently delete it. File versions may exist under Nextcloud's automatic version-management schedule.

08 / CHOICE & CONTROL

Consent, refusal, and withdrawal

Before AI data is sent

The App presents a separate AI Data Sharing authorization screen. Data is sent only after you actively select “Agree and Continue.”

If you refuse

Selecting “Not Now” prevents AI content from being sent. Local viewing, editing, file management, and other non-AI tools remain available.

Withdrawal

You may withdraw authorization in Me > AI Data & Privacy. This blocks new AI transmissions unless you later authorize them again.

Withdrawal and deletion are separate. Withdrawal stops future sharing; it does not automatically delete content already processed or retained in logs, remote storage, provider systems, or backups. Submit a deletion request under Section 9.
