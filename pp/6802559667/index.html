03 / DATA PROCESSING

Information the App processes

Camera images and selected photos

When you scan a sign, document, menu, product label, or scene, the App processes the camera frame or photo you select to detect and translate text. Camera frames that you do not save are used for the active translation session.

Recognized text and translations

The App processes text detected in an image or text you type or paste. Translation results may be displayed, copied, exported, or saved only when you choose those actions.

Language and App preferences

The App may store source and target language selections, display preferences, downloaded language resources, and other functional settings on your device so the App works as expected.

Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, attachments you choose to include, and related correspondence. The App does not require an account or collect your name, phone number, contacts, precise location, or social profile.

04 / PERMISSIONS

Device permissions

PermissionPurpose
CameraCapture live scenes, signs, documents, or other images for text recognition and translation.
PhotosImport a photo you select or save a translated image when you request it. We do not scan your entire photo library.
FilesOpen an image or supported file you select, or save/export a result to a location you choose.

You can refuse or revoke permissions in iOS Settings. Camera translation will not work without Camera access; photo import will not work without the relevant Photos or Files access. Typed-text translation remains available where supported.

05 / SHARING & THIRD PARTIES

What we do not collect or share

The App does not include user accounts, social sign-in, public profiles, public posting, user-to-user messaging, targeted advertising, cross-app tracking, or telephone/SMS marketing. We do not sell or rent photos, recognized text, translation content, or personal information.

Standard Apple services may be involved in installing the App, processing App Store transactions, managing permissions, or restoring an iCloud/device backup according to your Apple settings. Apple processes that information under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Sharing initiated by you. If you tap Copy, Share, Save, or Export, the destination App or service you select receives the content under its own privacy practices.

  • Delete saved items inside the App or from the storage location where you exported them.
  • Remove the App and its data through iOS to delete locally stored App content.
  • Manage device or iCloud backups in your Apple account settings.
  • Ask us to access, correct, or delete support correspondence by emailing contact@yuzhilintech.com with the subject “Offline Translator Privacy Request.”

We will complete a verified privacy request within 30 days, unless applicable law requires a different period.
